# Code of Conduct

Emaki is made for people who tell stories, and we want everyone who uses it or works on it to feel welcome: whatever their experience, background, identity, language, or the kind of stories they write.

## What we expect
- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to programming, to visual novels, or to English.
- Critique work, not people. Explain what's wrong and, when you can, how to fix it.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Respect other people's games and art. Don't share anyone's unreleased work, assets or private messages without their permission.
- Accept that maintainers sometimes say no to a feature, and that "not now" isn't "never".

## What isn't acceptable
- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery aimed at people in the community, and unwelcome advances.
- Threats, doxxing, or encouraging anyone else to do these things.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, or continuing after being asked to stop.

The stories people make with Emaki may deal with any subject; this code covers how we treat each other, not what games are about.

## Where it applies
In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews), and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting
If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer privately through [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/AbhishekNair050). Reports are kept confidential. You'll get a reply, and the maintainer will decide what to do, from a private word, to a warning, to removing comments, to banning someone from the project, depending on what happened and whether it has happened before.

This code is adapted from the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
