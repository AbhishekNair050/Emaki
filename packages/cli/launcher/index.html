Compression
PNG and BMP to WebP Pictures bigger than the game’s screen in both directions, made smaller. Panoramas are kept. WAV, FLAC, OGG and MP3 to Opus Voice files to Opus MP4, MOV, AVI and others to H.264

Only the exported copy is compressed: the files in assets stay as they are. Files that wouldn’t get smaller are left alone. Saved in the project’s export.json.
