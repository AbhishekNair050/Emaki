# Audio credits

All audio in this demo is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), so no attribution is required. We credit the creators anyway, with thanks. Files were trimmed, loudness-matched and re-encoded to MP3; nothing else was changed.

| File | Original | Author | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `theme_forget_me_not.mp3` | [Forget Me Not](https://opengameart.org/content/forget-me-not) (looped version) | Kistol | Loudness-matched |
| `storm_rain.mp3` | [Rain (loopable)](https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable), track 2 | Ylmir | Mono, loudness-matched |
| `shore_waves.mp3` | [Sea and river wave sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/sea-and-river-wave-sounds) (short version, recorded on the Vistula) | RandomMind | 40 s excerpt from 1:10, ends crossfaded into the start so it loops seamlessly; mono |
| `thunder.mp3` | [Rain + Long Thunder](https://opengameart.org/content/rain-long-thunder) | WuxiaScrub | The thunder roll (0:19.5 to 0:36), faded in and out; mono |
| `crank.mp3` | [Chain winch sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/chain-winch-sounds) ("Marker 2") | bart | Faded out; mono, loudness-matched |

Replacing these with your own is just a matter of changing the `audio` lines at the top of `story.vn`.
